Features

Small app.
Big lifts.

A diary by day

Every set lands under Today, tagged Push, Pull or Legs, with its own icon.

Fix it in a tap

Tap any set to edit it or bin it. Changed your mind? Undo.

Hears what you mean

It hears shoulder razors, it logs shoulder raises. Common mishearings are fixed for you.

Hands-free or hold to talk

Leave the mic on and use the wake word, or hold the big button in a noisy gym.

A ping, not a chat

A quick ping confirms each set. Turn sound off any time.

Space bar on desktop

On a laptop, press Space to turn the mic on or off.
